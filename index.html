Case study 01 · Foundational research

Seller Lifecycle

A shared framework for seller strategy.

SegmentationResearch adoptionStrategic planning

The question

Etsy lacked a shared model for how seller goals evolved over time and with operational scale. Teams alternated between one-size-fits-all assumptions and a hobbyist-versus-professional binary.

My approach

I identified gaps in prior segmentation and led a mixed-method initiative grounded in growth motivation and behavioral data. I partnered with Analytics, Product, and external research firms, presented to the CEO and CPO, and established a three-year refresh cadence after the foundational work in 2019.

The decision it enabled

We mapped product initiatives and investments to lifecycle stages, enabling more targeted prioritization and evaluation. As the segmentation evolved, I worked with Analytics leadership to preserve the strategic narrative while integrating new evidence.

The central insight

Success creates motivation to invest.

Growth in shop sales became the primary organizing principle for seller product strategy.

Case study 02 · Product innovation

Gift Mode

Reframing shopping around the gift recipient.

Generative researchConcept developmentExecutive alignment

The question

Many Etsy buyers came for gifting, but recommendations, prompts, and discovery reflected the shopper’s preferences rather than the recipient’s needs.

My approach

I originated and led an innovation pod of two designers, two researchers, and an analyst, with direct visibility to the CPO. I led concept development, naming, validation, and opportunity sizing, then co-developed a unified Product and Marketing brief for the CEO.

The decision it enabled

The behavioral framework aligned Product and Marketing around one customer premise, secured executive approval for a cross-functional initiative, and established a repeatable innovation model.

The central insight

Put the recipient at the center.

Gifting is a distinct behavioral mode requiring tailored discovery, personalization, and messaging.

Case study 03 · Monetization strategy

Offsite Ads

Resolving a sensitive participation decision.

Mixed methodsChoice modelingPrice sensitivity

The question

Etsy needed broad seller participation in Offsite Ads to offer a compelling inventory feed for Google Product Listing Ads. The existing opt-in model constrained coverage and revenue potential.

My approach

I led qualitative interviews and choice modeling with a mixed-methods team and the CFO as executive sponsor. The work connected market strategy with the experience sellers would encounter in-product.

The decision it enabled

I drove adoption of a tiered model: mandatory but flexible participation for high-volume sellers, with opt-out flexibility for smaller sellers. It balanced the need for broad coverage with differing seller sensitivities.

The central insight

One rule would not fit every seller.

Distinct price-sensitivity thresholds made seller agency and marketplace trust central to program design.
